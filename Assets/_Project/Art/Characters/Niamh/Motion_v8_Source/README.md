# Niamh Motion v8 source parts

Copied from the versioned design source `TechArtAsset/Characters/Niamh/Motion_v8/parts/` on 2026-11-14. Six RGBA PNGs share the original 327 × 699 canvas and are intended to align without per-layer cropping. `motion_preview_spec.json` records offline study pivots and timing. The GIF/contact sheet in `Docs/References/Niamh_Motion_v8/` are reference previews only.

On 2026-11-14 Unity imported these files as full-canvas Sprites at 100 PPU with bottom-center pivots and uncompressed alpha. The reversible prefab and seven clips are in the adjacent `Motion_v8_Trial/` directory. The owner tried this six-piece rigid trial and reported it basically OK apart from some stutter; its cause remains unverified. Keep each part's dimensions and common origin; do not trim transparent borders or overwrite the static source. Follow `Docs/Niamh_Motion_v8_Unity_Handoff.md` for implementation, evidence and rollback. Limitations: no separate knees, elbows, split cape, or multi-part hair.
